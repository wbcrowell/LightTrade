The Light Trade

The gist

Get an 8-card hand of old light fixtures to re-use, recycle, or landfill

Each card has a value based on the materials it contains, which changes when market prices change

Maximize the value of your hand by swapping with other players, drawing a Chance card, or cashing out

Cashing out fixes the value of your hand and ends play

The game ends when every player has cashed out

Developed byTeam EurekaDeveloped forFND2007 Community Practice
All creative elements of this game, including rules and graphics, were created by human beings. The tech platform upon which it runs—which manages the 'internal plumbing' of the game, including user communication, maintenance of state, scoring, and randomization—is human-designed and build with AI-assisted tools.

How to play

Click on any card to view it or swap it with another player

Click anywhere to continue
